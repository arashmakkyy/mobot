# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Mobot to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience,
identity, background, or ability. We pledge to make participation respectful
and harassment-free.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but take responsibility for the impact of your words.
- Give and accept technical feedback gracefully.
- Respect different viewpoints and use cases.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted attention.
- Discriminatory or derogatory comments about identity, background, or ability.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Threats, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Encouraging or defending any of the above.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other forum where someone is representing Mobot. It also
applies when someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the
maintainers through GitHub's **Security → Report a vulnerability** flow at
<https://github.com/arashmakkyy/mobot/security/advisories/new>. Include what
happened, where, and any relevant links. Reports will be reviewed as promptly
as possible and handled with discretion.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently ban participants for behavior they consider inappropriate,
threatening, or harmful. Enforcement decisions will be proportional and
documented privately where possible.
