## Fonts

### Vazirmatn

Copyright 2015 The Vazirmatn Project Authors
(<https://github.com/rastikerdar/vazirmatn>).

Vazirmatn is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1. The full
license text is distributed at [`assets/fonts/OFL.txt`](assets/fonts/OFL.txt)
and is also available at <https://scripts.sil.org/OFL>.

The font is embedded as WOFF2 data in `css/mobot.css` so that Persian text
renders correctly when the simulator is injected into a page.
